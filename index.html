Coverage

What do you need?

Eight needs, 36 kinds of place, one map. The same tiles open the app.

Bathrooms & water

Restrooms, showers, drinking water, gyms and laundromats

Power & Wi-Fi

Outlets, charging, libraries, cafés and Wi-Fi hotspots

Camping

Free & paid campsites, overnight parking and rest areas

Gas & auto

Cheapest gas, propane, dump stations and mechanics

Food

Pantries, free meals and markets

Help

Shelters, benefits offices, clinics, cooling centers, pharmacies and post offices

Money

Banks and ATMs

Things to do

Trails, bike trails, gear shops, events, theme & water parks, movies, geocaches, rivers and waterfalls

Each place carries what matters on the road: hours and whether it's open now, free or paid, ADA, 24 h, Wi-Fi, laundry, showers, fuel prices by grade, EBT acceptance, event dates, a street-level or aerial photo, and community notes and tips.

Product

Tap what you need. See the closest places. Go.

Finding places never needs an account, and every lookup runs on the phone, so the answer is there with or without signal.

  1. 1

    Tap what you need

    Pick a need, or narrow it to one kind of place: restrooms, showers or water.

  2. 2

    See the closest places

    On the map and in a list, closest first, or cheapest first for gas, by grade.

  3. 3

    Go

    Hours, photos and directions on every place, with a street-level look before you get there.

Trip tools

Plan the route. Check the place.

Places along your drive, a shortlist that works offline, and a real look at a spot before you go.

Along a route

Pick a destination and see up to 100 places within ½, 1 or 5 miles of the first 300 miles, in order along the way.

Saved places

Star a place to keep it on the phone: up to 500, listed closest first, offline, with no account.

Share

Send a place as a link that opens it in VANLYFE, Apple Maps, Google Maps or OpenStreetMap.

Photos nearby

Up to 12 photos taken within 150 m of the place, from Wikimedia Commons, Flickr and street-level imagery, pinned on the map where they were taken.

Look around, satellite and 3D

Google's Look around, satellite and 3D views of the place, plus its Google Maps card, right on the place card.

Tips from travelers

Public, anonymous tips with helpful votes and an optional “stayed overnight” date; reports hide them.

Also new: Search Google nearby when a place isn't in the list, Open now and Hide closed, a Free filter for camps, and laundromats, rest areas, pharmacies, post offices, rivers and waterfalls on the map.

Beyond the map

More than pins

The forecast, live layers and alerts, what's on this week, and where your benefits work, for wherever the map is.

Today

NWS forecast and alerts, air quality and pollen, sunrise, sunset, tonight's sky and tides, wildfires within 100 miles, earthquakes and local radio.

Live map layers

Rain radar, lightning, hurricanes and wind; road cameras, earthquakes and a crime heatmap; public lands, night lights and rivers.

Alerts

Opt-in notifications for NWS warnings, new fires within 25 miles and unhealthy air where the phone last was.

Events

Everything happening this week within 50 miles, by day, as a list, a photo grid or a map. Tap one and it opens on the map.

Benefits

Stores and restaurants that take SNAP near you, plus plain-language guides to CalFresh, General Relief, Medi-Cal, Oregon SNAP and the Oregon Health Plan in five languages.

Travelers

Opt-in: show yourself to other travelers who share, rounded to about 1 km unless you choose Exact. Report or hide anyone.

Why it wins

Works where the signal doesn't

Each state's places download once, a few MB, and every lookup runs on the phone with SQLite. Save map areas before you leave signal.

Offline-first by design

Places, filters and saved map areas keep working with no signal, and the national overview fills the map as you zoom out to the whole country.

Open data, continuously rebuilt

35+ sources, deduplicated by a scheduled ETL and published as static shards on a CDN. The phone does the query, so a lookup costs nothing to serve.

Photos before you go

Recreation.gov and The Dyrt photos, USDA aerials for every place, street-level photos (Street View, Mapillary, KartaView, Panoramax), Wikimedia Commons and Flickr photos nearby, and community photos.

Five languages

English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese and Filipino, across the app, the guides and every category name.

Trust

No ads. No tracking. Nothing to sell you.

Read the full privacy policy.

Your location stays on your phone

Unless you opt in to Travelers. Weather requests use coordinates rounded to 0.01°.

Accounts are optional

Finding places never needs one. Sign in only to add spots, post photos or share your position.

Community moderation

Report a place, a photo or a traveler, block uploaders, and suggest edits that go through review.

Self-hosted backend

Accounts and community posts live on our own server, with Play data-safety and App Store privacy labels filled out truthfully.

Accessible

Automated accessibility audits on every screen: contrast, text size, touch targets, VoiceOver and TalkBack labels.

Delete any time

Community › Settings › Account › Delete account removes your account, profile, messages, photos and traveler profile, or ask by email.

Get the app

Free on Android and iPhone

VANLYFE is in beta. Android installs straight from this page; iPhone installs through Apple's TestFlight.

Android

Android 8.0 or newer on a 64-bit phone

Download for Android
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns about the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Tap Open when the download finishes. If that button is gone, open vanlyfe.apk from Downloads in your Files app.
  3. If your phone says it isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. If Play Protect warns about the app, tap More details, then Install anyway.

To update, download and install again from this page; the new version replaces the old one and keeps your saved places. If you already have VANLYFE from Google Play, uninstall it first.

iPhone

iOS 17 or newer, through Apple's TestFlight app

Join the iPhone beta
  1. On your iPhone, install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Come back to this page, tap Join the iPhone beta, then View in TestFlight.
  3. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install.

New versions show up in TestFlight with an Update button. Each beta version stops working 90 days after it comes out.
