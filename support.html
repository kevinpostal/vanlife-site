Support

VANLYFE finds what you need on the road: restrooms, showers, drinking water, power and Wi-Fi, free and paid camping, gas prices, propane and dump stations, food pantries and free meals, EBT stores, shelters, clinics and more, anywhere in the US. Place data downloads once per state and then works offline, and the map fills in as you zoom out, up to the whole country. The Today tab shows the local forecast and weather alerts, wildfires within 100 miles, sunrise and sunset, and today's events nearby.

Frequently asked questions

Why does VANLYFE ask for my location?

To sort places by distance, center the map on you, and show your local weather on the Today tab. Your location is never sent to us; the Today tab sends it to the National Weather Service to get your forecast (see the privacy policy). You can still browse the map and search by place name without it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The places for a state download the first time you look there and stay on your phone. To keep the map itself offline, save an area in Settings › Offline maps before you lose signal.

How do I add a spot or a photo?

Create a free account in Settings › Account. Then tap + (Add a spot) at the top of the list to submit a new place, or open a place and tap Add photo or Suggest edit. New spots and edits are reviewed before they appear for everyone; photos appear right away.

How do I report a photo?

Open the photo full screen, tap ••• in the top-left corner, then choose Report. The photo is hidden for everyone immediately while we review it.

How do I report wrong information about a place?

Open the place and tap Report problem, then choose what's wrong. You don't need an account. The place stays on the map while we review it.

How do I hide someone's photos?

Open one of their photos full screen, tap •••, then choose Hide photos from this person. Their photos stop showing on your phone.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings › Account and tap Delete account. Your account and your photos are removed.

Moderation

We review every report within 24 hours. Photos that break the rules are removed, and accounts that post them are removed. Everyone who posts agrees to the Terms of Use.
