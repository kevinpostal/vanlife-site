Android

Download for Android

Needs Android 8.0 or newer on a 64-bit phone.

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns about the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Tap Open when the download finishes, or open vanlyfe.apk from Files › Downloads.
  3. If your phone asks, let your browser install unknown apps: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about the app, tap More details, then Install anyway.

To update, download and install again from this page; the new version replaces the old one and keeps your saved places. If you already have VANLYFE from Google Play, uninstall it first.

iPhone

Join the iPhone beta

Needs iOS 17 or newer. Installs through Apple's TestFlight app.

  1. On your iPhone, install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Come back to this page, tap Join the iPhone beta, then View in TestFlight.
  3. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install.

New versions show up in TestFlight with an Update button. Each beta version stops working 90 days after it comes out.
